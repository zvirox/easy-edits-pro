# Commercial licence — Easy Edits Pro

Easy Edits Pro is open source under AGPL-3.0-or-later. This file does **not**
grant any commercial rights, and it is not itself the agreement. The real
commercial licence is a separately signed contract between the copyright
holder and the licensee.

## Who needs one?

- Companies embedding Easy Edits Pro in a **closed-source product**
- Anyone operating Easy Edits Pro as a **hosted service whose terms cannot
  comply with AGPL section 13** source availability
- Teams wanting **support, SLA and warranty** coverage from the maintainers

Individuals, hobbyists, researchers and open-source projects never need to
apply — the AGPL covers you (see [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md)).

## What the commercial licence covers

- Use, modification and distribution of Easy Edits Pro in a proprietary
  product without copyleft duties
- Negotiated scope: per developer seat, per product, per hosted deployment,
  or per render/minute
- Optional support and SLA schedule

It does **not** cover:

- Third-party components, which keep their own licences ([NOTICE](NOTICE),
  `licenses/`)
- Codec patents (H.264/HEVC/AAC and similar) — the licensee's responsibility
- The "Easy Edits Pro" name or logo ([TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md))
- Non-free model weights — those are never bundled; see
  [docs/model-licences.md](docs/model-licences.md)

## Requesting a licence

Contact the copyright holder with: your company name, intended use
(embedded product / hosted service / support), estimated deployment scale
(users, renders or processed minutes per month), and your contact details.

- **info@zvirox.in** (primary)
- **zvirox@proton.me** (alternative)

We aim to reply to licence enquiries within five business days. Signed
agreements are exchanged by e-mail; on request we can route through your
procurement process.
