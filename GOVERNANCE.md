# Governance

Easy Edits Pro is led by its founder and lead maintainer, **zvirox**. This page
says how decisions are made today, in plain terms. It will change as the
project grows.

## Roles

- **Lead maintainer:** sets direction and priorities, owns releases, the
  licensing model and the trademark, and has the final say.
- **Maintainers:** review and merge pull requests in the areas they own
  (see `.github/CODEOWNERS`). Invited by the lead maintainer.
- **Contributors:** anyone who opens an issue, a discussion or a pull request.

## How someone becomes a maintainer

After sustained, good-quality contributions (typically several merged pull
requests, helpful reviews, and reliable behaviour under the
[Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)), the lead maintainer may invite them to
own an area. Maintainers who are inactive for six months may be moved to
emeritus status, with thanks.

## How decisions are made

- Small changes: one maintainer approval and passing CI.
- Larger or user-visible changes: open an issue or Discussion first. We aim for
  rough consensus; the lead maintainer decides if there is none.
- Changes to the licence, the CLA, the trademark policy or the security model
  are decided by the lead maintainer after public discussion where possible.

## Licensing

The project is dual licensed (AGPL-3.0-or-later and a commercial licence); see
[LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md). Contributors keep their copyright and accept the
[CLA](CLA.md) so both licences can be offered.

## Conflicts

Disagreements are settled in the open, respectfully, on the issue or
discussion. Conduct problems go to **info@zvirox.in** as described in the Code
of Conduct.
