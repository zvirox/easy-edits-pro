## Forks

Forking is allowed under the AGPL, but forks must rename the product. If
your fork diverges, pick a new name ("Classic Edits Pro" style reuse that
still reads as the official one is not ok). Forks must keep the
[LICENSE](LICENSE) and [NOTICE](NOTICE) intact, and must remove or
visibly separate official hosted-branding assets.
